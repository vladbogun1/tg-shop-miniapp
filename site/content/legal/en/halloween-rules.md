# Halloween Hunt giveaway rules

A Halloween prize draw among ChiSetup customers and viewers. The winners are picked live on stream.

## 1. Organiser

**Sole proprietor Maksym Solokha**, the seller of the ChiSetup shop (see the [Terms of use](/terms)). Contacts are on the [Contacts](/contacts) page.

## 2. Dates

- Pumpkins and orders count from **Friday 30.10.2026 00:00** to **Sunday 01.11.2026 18:00** (Kyiv time).
- The draws are live on **Sunday 01.11.2026 at 19:00** on [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@lessons_maxsolch) and [Kick](https://kick.com/maxsolkh).

## 3. How to enter

Sign in with Telegram on chisetup.com.ua or open the shop's Mini App in Telegram — otherwise we cannot contact the winner.

There are **two separate draws**:

**Pumpkin draw (free).** Pumpkins pop up on the pages of the site and the Mini App from time to time — tap one to collect it. Up to 8 pumpkins a day.

- Everyone with at least **1 pumpkin** takes part.
- **A full set is 15 pumpkins.** A partial set gives 1 ticket per pumpkin (1–14), a full set at least 100 tickets. All partial sets together hold at most 10% of the chance: a full set weighs more when there are many partial ones. So a few pumpkins give a very small chance.
- **Code words.** During the event (until Nov 1, 18:00) code words may be announced on TikTok and Kick — enter them in the event panel to get extra pumpkins. Each word works a limited number of times, once per person.

**Purchase draw.** Customers whose orders during the event add up to **500 UAH or more**.

- 1 ticket per full 500 UAH, up to 5 tickets.
- Orders placed during the event and paid or accepted by the shop count; cancelled, rejected and refunded amounts do not.

The pumpkin draw is free, no purchase needed. Whether you are in is shown in the 🎃 panel on the site and in the Mini App.

## 4. The draw

- **6 prizes in each draw**, one per round. One person can win only one prize within a draw, but can win in both draws.
- A participant's chance is proportional to their number of tickets.
- Before the stream the list of each draw is frozen and a fingerprint (sha256) of a secret number and the list is shown on screen. That number, not the host, decides the winners. After the draw the number and the list can be checked.
- The prizes are announced separately before the stream.

## 5. Claiming a prize

- The organiser messages the winner on Telegram (through the shop's bot) within a day after the stream.
- If a winner does not answer within **48 hours** or declines the prize, the round is drawn again among the remaining participants.
- The prize is shipped with Nova Poshta at the organiser's expense. No cash equivalent.

## 6. Not eligible

Administrators and staff of the shop, test accounts. The organiser may exclude a participant who collected pumpkins with a script, a bot or several accounts of one person.

## 7. Personal data

The stream shows only the first name, the initial of the last name and a partly hidden Telegram username (e.g. "Oleh K. @ole\*\*\*"). The full data is seen only by the organiser and used only to contact the winner and ship the prize. See the [Privacy policy](/privacy).
