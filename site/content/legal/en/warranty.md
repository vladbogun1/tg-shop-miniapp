# Warranty

All goods except consumables are covered by the store's warranty. The warranty period is shown on the product page. If no period is shown, the warranty is valid for **6 months** from the day you receive the parcel.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects that appear during normal use. For example:

- a key, switch, or mouse button doesn't register or double-clicks;
- the connection (wired, 2.4 GHz, Bluetooth) doesn't work, or the battery doesn't charge;
- the backlight, sensor, microphone, or one of the speakers doesn't work;
- the mouse pad surface peels off, or the mechanism of a chair or desk breaks under normal load.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Physical damage: drops, impacts, cracks, signs that the housing has been opened.
- Damage from liquids, moisture, or high temperatures.
- Signs of self-repair, resoldering, or replaced parts (except for replacing keycaps and switches on hot-swap keyboards, as long as this did not damage the PCB).
- Normal wear and tear: worn keycaps, skates, and feet, or a mouse pad surface worn down by long-term use.
- Consumables and accessories: skates, keycaps, cables, sleeves — only the 14-day return rules apply to them.
- Defects of discounted goods that are stated in the product description.
- Problems caused by unofficial software or firmware.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Message us in the order chat on the website or in the Telegram bot. Include your order number.
2. Describe the problem and attach a photo or video that shows the defect.
3. We will tell you whether the problem can be fixed with a settings change or a firmware update. If not, we will send you instructions on how to ship the item.
4. Ship the item via Nova Poshta with all its contents and, if possible, in its original packaging.
5. After inspection, we repair or replace the item. If repair or replacement is not possible, we issue a refund.

Inspection takes up to **14 days** from the day we receive the item. If the manufacturer needs to examine it, this may take longer — we will let you know in chat.

## Who pays for shipping

- If the warranty claim is confirmed, the store pays for shipping both ways.
- If the case is not covered by the warranty (damage caused by the Buyer, wear and tear), the Buyer pays for shipping. Paid repair is possible by agreement.

## Your statutory rights

This warranty does not limit the rights granted to you by the Law of Ukraine “On Consumer Rights Protection.” Where the terms on this page differ from the law in your favor, the terms on this page apply. Where they are less favorable to you, the law applies.
