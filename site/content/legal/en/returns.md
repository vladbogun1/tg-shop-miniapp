# Returns and Exchanges

You can return or exchange an item within **14 days** of the day you received the parcel. To do so, message us in the order chat (on the website or in the Telegram bot) and include your order number.

## Returning goods of proper quality

If an item simply doesn't suit you — because of its shape, color, size, or any other reason — you can return it provided all of the following conditions are met:

- no more than 14 days have passed since the day you received it;
- the item has not been used: there are no signs of use, scratches, or dirt;
- its original condition, complete set of contents, factory packaging, protective films, seals, and tags are preserved;
- the item is not on the list of goods that cannot be returned under the laws of Ukraine.

The Buyer pays for shipping the item back to us. We refund the price of the item after we receive and inspect the parcel.

## Returning defective goods

If an item arrived damaged, incomplete, or with a manufacturing defect:

1. Inspect the parcel at the Nova Poshta branch before paying for and accepting it. If the packaging is damaged or the item is clearly broken, have a damage report drawn up with the branch staff and refuse to accept the parcel.
2. If the defect shows up later, message us in the order chat and attach a photo or video of the defect.
3. We will agree with you on an exchange, repair, or refund. The store pays for shipping the defective item both ways.

For more on warranty cases, see the [Warranty](/warranty) page.

## Discounted goods

Items in the “Discounted” category are sold at a reduced price because of defects stated in the product description. A discounted item cannot be returned because of those defects. Defects not stated in the description are covered by the general rules on this page.

## Refusing a parcel and prepayment

If you chose to pay with a 100 UAH prepayment plus cash on delivery, the prepayment is credited toward the order total. If you do not collect the parcel or refuse it at the branch without a valid reason (the item has no defects and matches the order), the prepayment is not refunded: it covers shipping to you and back.

## How we issue refunds

- Online card payments are refunded to the same card you paid with. Money paid by cash on delivery is refunded to a card you specify in the order chat.
- We process the refund within 14 days of the day we receive the returned item. How long the funds take to reach your card depends on your bank, usually up to 3–7 banking days.
- We refund the amount you actually paid for the item. The cost of shipping to you is not refunded if the item was of proper quality.

## Canceling an order before shipment

As long as an order has not been paid for or shipped, you can cancel it yourself in your account or in the Mini App. An order not paid within 24 hours is canceled automatically. If you have already paid for the order, message us in chat — we will cancel it manually and refund the money to the card you paid with.
