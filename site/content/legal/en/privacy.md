# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data we collect on the chisetup.com.ua website and in the store's Telegram bot and Mini App, why we need it, and how we protect it.

**Data controller (personal data owner):** Sole proprietor (FOP) Maksym Andriiovych Solokha, tax ID (RNOKPP) 3547612413. For any questions about your data, contact us through the order chat or using the details on the [Contacts](/contacts) page.

## What data we collect

**From Telegram, when you sign in to the website or open the Mini App:**

- your Telegram ID, first name, last name, and username — as they appear in your Telegram profile;
- your Telegram interface language, Telegram Premium status, and a link to your profile photo.

We do not receive your phone number from Telegram.

**When you place an order:**

- the recipient's full name and phone number;
- the city and the Nova Poshta branch or parcel locker;
- the order contents, total amount, promo code, comment, and selected payment method.

**When you chat with us and pay:**

- messages and images in the order chat;
- payment data from monobank: amount, payment status, payment method (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay), and the masked card number (only the first 6 and last 4 digits).

**Technical data:**

- IP address and browser type — to protect sign-ins and the list of your devices in your account;
- your last IP address and approximate location based on it (country, city) — for security and visit statistics; kept for up to 90 days;
- anonymized statistics of clicks in the interface (which button was clicked, without the contents of any fields) — kept for 30 days to help us improve the store.

We do not receive or store full card details (number, expiration date, CVV): they are entered on the secure monobank payment page.

## Why we need this data

- To process, confirm, ship, and deliver your order.
- To send you order status updates, tracking numbers (TTN), and manager replies in Telegram.
- To verify payments and process returns or warranty repairs.
- To protect your account: sign-in confirmation via the bot, a device list, and signing out of all devices.
- To keep sales records as required by law.
- To send you store news and promotions via the Telegram bot. You can opt out by blocking the bot or messaging us.

## Who we share data with

- **Nova Poshta** — the recipient's full name, phone number, and branch, to create and deliver the parcel.
- **Telegram** — bot messages are delivered through Telegram's servers.
- **monobank (JSC Universal Bank)** — the order amount and number, to accept card payments; you enter your payment details directly on the monobank page.
- **Hosting** — data is stored on our server in Oracle Cloud.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertising networks or any other third parties, except as required by law.

## Cookies and browser local storage

The website uses only essential cookies:

- session and sign-in refresh cookies — to keep you signed in for up to 30 days;
- a temporary sign-in confirmation cookie — valid for 5 minutes;
- language and theme settings and cart contents — stored in your browser (localStorage).

The website has no advertising or third-party analytics cookies. If we ever add them, we will ask for your consent separately.

## How long we keep data

- Order data and correspondence — for as long as you use the store and for **3 years** after your last order, for accounting, returns, and warranty purposes.
- Payment data — together with the order.
- Sign-in sessions — 30 days from last use; you can end them earlier in your account.
- Click statistics — 30 days.
- Last IP address and approximate location — up to 90 days from your last visit.

## Your rights

Under the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection,” you can:

- find out what data of yours we store and get a copy of it;
- correct inaccurate data;
- request that your data be deleted if we are not required by law to keep it;
- withdraw your consent to receive mailings;
- file a complaint with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or with a court.

To exercise any of these rights, message us. We will respond within 30 days.

## Data protection

The website runs only over a secure HTTPS connection. Session cookies are not accessible to page scripts. Only you and the store's administrators have access to your orders; administrator actions are logged.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with the date of the update. We will notify you of significant changes via the Telegram bot.
