# Delivery and Payment

## Delivery

**Nova Poshta — anywhere in Ukraine.** At checkout, choose where the parcel goes:

- **to a branch** — right on the map or from the list;
- **to a parcel locker** — for small parcels: a locker only takes a parcel up to a certain weight and size (the limit is shown on the map); a bigger one we will offer to send to a branch;
- **by courier to your address** — street, building and flat (if this option is available at checkout).

We ship within **1–2 business days** after the order is confirmed; on average, a parcel takes 1–3 days to arrive. The tracking number (TTN) will appear in your account and will also be sent to you by the Telegram bot, where you can also see where the parcel is now. When the parcel arrives at the branch or locker, the bot will let you know.

Shipping is **not part of the order total**: you pay it separately to Nova Poshta on receipt, at their rates — the shop does not take this money. An approximate shipping price is shown at checkout; the final one is calculated by Nova Poshta. If you pay part of the amount on receipt, Nova Poshta also charges its own fee for transferring that money. Exceptions, if any, are stated in the payment method description.

**Pickup.** Collect your order from the store's pickup point: **Kharkiv; please confirm the address with the manager in chat; opening hours — 8:00–20:00**. Once the order is confirmed, we will let you know in chat when it is ready for pickup.

## Payment

Payment is made **online by card via monobank** (plata by mono): Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or in the monobank app. After you place an order, a secure monobank payment page opens. Card details are entered only there — the store never sees or stores them.

The payment options are the same on the website and in the Telegram bot. Which ones are available right now and how much the prepayment is are shown at checkout.

### Full payment online

- You pay the full order amount by card right after placing the order.
- At the Nova Poshta branch, you pay only for shipping at the carrier's rate.

### Online prepayment + cash on delivery

- You pay a prepayment by card right after placing the order — its amount is shown in the payment option.
- You pay the remaining amount at the Nova Poshta branch on receipt (cash on delivery).
- The prepayment is credited toward the price of the goods. If you refuse the parcel without a valid reason, the prepayment covers shipping both ways and is non-refundable — see [Returns and Exchanges](/returns).

### Pay on delivery

- If this option is available, you pay nothing online: you pay the whole amount at the Nova Poshta branch on receipt.
- There is a limit on the order amount and on the number of unfinished orders with this payment option per buyer — both are shown at checkout.
- A pay-on-delivery order is confirmed by a manager by hand; if it is not confirmed within the time shown at checkout, it is cancelled automatically and the goods go back on sale.

### Payment deadline

- The order must be paid **within 24 hours** of placing it. If payment is not received, the order is canceled automatically.
- If you closed the payment page or the payment failed, pay for the order again using the “Pay” button on the order page in your account — with the same card or a different one.
- After payment, the manager checks that the goods are in stock and confirms the order. The payment and order status are shown in your account, and the Telegram bot will send you a notification.

### Refunds

If an order is canceled or the goods are returned, the money is refunded **to the same card** you paid with. How long the funds take to arrive depends on your bank, usually up to 3–7 banking days. Terms for returning goods are on the [Returns and Exchanges](/returns) page.

## Promo codes

A promo code can be entered in the cart or at checkout. The discount applies to the goods, not to shipping. Promo codes with a limited number of uses are reserved for you for 30 minutes while you complete your order.
