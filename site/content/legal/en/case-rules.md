# «Gift cases» promotion rules

These rules describe the ChiSetup advertising promotion in which an order above a certain amount comes with a gift — a case with a device or a discount. The promotion applies to orders placed on the chisetup.com.ua website and in the store's Telegram bot and Mini App.

## 1. Organiser

**Sole proprietor (FOP) Maksym Andriiovych Solokha**, tax ID (RNOKPP) 3547612413 — the seller of the ChiSetup store (see the [Terms of Use](/terms)). Contact details are on the [Contacts](/contacts) page.

## 2. Period

The promotion runs indefinitely, until the organiser cancels it. The organiser may change these rules or end the promotion by publishing a new version on this page. Orders placed earlier are governed by the version in force when they were placed.

The promotion may be paused — the «Case tiers and gifts» block below then says so, and new orders do not get cases.

## 3. Taking part

- Taking part is free. Cases are not sold: you cannot buy a case, an extra opening or another try.
- Product prices do not depend on the promotion: a product costs the same with or without a case.
- An order gets a case when its total after all discounts (a promo code, a bundle) reaches the threshold of one of the tiers. If several thresholds are reached, the highest tier applies.
- One order — one case.

## 5. How the gift is determined

- Every case contains a gift — there are no empty cases.
- The gift is determined at random when the order is placed, among the gifts of that tier that are in stock. Rare, epic, legendary and gold gifts come up rarely.
- The gift is determined by the store's server. The opening animation only shows the gift already determined and does not affect the result.

## 6. Choice: a guaranteed discount or a case

On tiers where this is offered, before placing the order the buyer chooses one of two gift options: a guaranteed discount on this order (the percentage and the maximum amount are shown above) or a case. The choice is made before the order is placed and cannot be changed afterwards.

## 7. Opening the case and receiving the gift

- The case opens in the order once it is paid online — in full or the prepayment. Until then the case stays closed. A case can be opened once.
- A device is added to the same order free of charge and ships in the same parcel.
- A discount is given as a personal promo code for the next order. The code can be used once and is valid for a limited time (shown for each tier above). A code not used in time expires.

## 8. Swapping the gift for a discount

If the case contains a product similar to one the buyer already has (the same product or a product of the same category from their orders), the gift can be swapped once for a discount promo code worth part of the gift's value (the percentage is shown above). The swap is possible until the order ships; after the swap the device is removed from the order.

## 9. Cash equivalent

Gifts — devices and discounts — cannot be exchanged for money, and no cash equivalent of a gift is paid.

## 10. Cancellation and return of the order

- If the order is cancelled before the case is opened (by the buyer, by the store or automatically for non-payment), the case is void and cannot be opened. The device gift is not shipped in that case.
- If the order is cancelled or returned in full, an unused promo code from the case (a discount or a swap) is deactivated, and the device gift is returned together with the order.
- The guaranteed discount is part of the order price: on a return, the money actually paid for the products is refunded.

## 11. Taxes

A gift is income of its recipient. Under the Tax Code of Ukraine, gifts worth up to 25% of the minimum monthly wage (UAH 2,161.75 in 2026) are not taxed. If a gift is worth more, the taxes on the excess are paid by the organiser as the tax agent in accordance with the Tax Code of Ukraine.

## 12. Other

- The promotion is an advertising campaign and is not a lottery or gambling: taking part requires no separate payment, and product prices do not depend on it.
- By placing an order that takes part in the promotion, the buyer agrees to these rules.
- Questions about the promotion: ask in the order chat or via [Contacts](/contacts).
