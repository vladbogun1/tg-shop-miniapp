- Mood: fun spooky Halloween night for a gaming gear shop stream, not gore, not horror-scary; cozy-creepy, energetic.

- Strong silhouettes, dramatic rim light from orange and violet sources, dark vignette edges.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos other than the triangle on the jersey, no UI, no watermarks, no frames or borders.
